# 🌟 The Moment Building Became My Superpower

**Date/Period**: 2019 - Cabin Building Phase

**Life Stage**: Early 20s, figuring out my path
**Emotional Impact**: Life-changing confidence boost
**Crystal Type**: Victory

## 🎬 The Story

I was standing in the middle of what would become my cabin, just a frame of studs and dreams. My hands were torn up from the work, my back ached, but something clicked. 

This wasn't just building - this was my brain finally finding its language. Every angle I could see in 3D, every problem had a solution I could touch and fix. The same brain that struggled with traditional school was now engineering spaces that actually worked.

I remember calling my mum and saying "I think I found what I'm actually good at." Not just building, but thinking in dimensions that made sense to me.

## 💫 What This Taught Me

My different way of thinking wasn't a limitation - it was a specialized tool waiting for the right job. Visual-spatial thinking is a superpower when you're creating physical things.

## 🔗 How It Connects

This confidence from building led to me believing I could tackle tech projects, which led to AI, which led to where I am now. The cabin was the foundation for everything.

## 🎯 Why This Matters for Others

Every neurodivergent person has a domain where their brain suddenly makes perfect sense. For me it was building. For others it might be coding, art, music, or something totally unexpected.
